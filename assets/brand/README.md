# Zelos brand assets

The approved September 12, 2026 logo: the three-wing mark and lowercase wordmark.

- Use the white transparent PNGs on dark surfaces and black PNGs on light surfaces. Keep their proportions.
- The wordmark belongs in navigation, welcome and About screens; use the wing alone for small avatars and app icons.
- `zelos-icon-180.png` is the iPhone home-screen icon. The 192/512 exports provide square app icons; the operating system supplies their outer mask.
- `zelos-desktop-icon.png` and `zelos-app-icon.svg` include a rounded black tile with transparent outer padding.
- `zelos-favicon.svg` is a small rounded browser icon; the 32px PNG is its fallback.
- SVG artwork masters preserve the supplied raster source in an SVG mask; these are not traced vector outlines. PNGs are compact exports rendered from those masters.

Source images were supplied by the user. The white-background wordmark and black-background standalone mark were used; checkerboard backgrounds in the other two references were not treated as transparency.
