# Meal serving photos

These are illustrative, licensed serving photos for Zelos meal cards. They are not photographs of a user’s exact generated recipe. Recipe ingredients, quantities, allergens and preparation remain the source of truth.

All files are bundled JPEGs, delivered by Zelos from the Spark. No user data was used in source searches, no model generated these assets, and displaying a photo requires no external image request.

Each photo was downloaded at 800 pixels wide using the source CDN’s supported size settings, and visually inspected on 2026-09-12. `manifest.json` records original source pages, creators, licenses, dimensions, SHA-256 checksums, and suggested `object-position` values for cropped cards.

## Matching notes

Use a photo only for a corresponding meal family. Fish and chicken photos must never serve as general vegetable or unknown-meal fallbacks. The vegetable-bowl photo is a vegan salad bowl; the vegetable-stir-fry photo includes grains in a vegetable pan. Photos may depict optional toppings or ingredients different from a generated recipe, so the interface should identify them as illustrative serving photos.

Unmatched recipes use a neutral vegetables-on-a-cutting-board image labeled “Kitchen inspiration”, rather than an unrelated prepared meal.

The expanded library includes chia pudding, cottage cheese, shakshuka, French toast, muesli, baked oats, ricotta toast, vegetable sandwiches and wraps, bean tacos, quesadillas, pureed vegetable soup, vegetable noodles, curry, risotto, stuffed peppers, and meatballs. Sandwich and wrap photos show cheese or cheese-like fillings and are not used for explicitly vegan or dairy-free recipes. Meatballs are reserved for recipes with meat; soup and noodle dish forms take priority over minor protein ingredients.

Protein additions show cod fillet, grilled beef steak, pork tenderloin, chicken salad and an egg sandwich. Fillet and steak photographs are not used for ground meat, meatballs, soup, sandwiches, wraps or tacos. The egg sandwich is not used for tuna. Vegetable plates and pans may illustrate compatible cooked vegetable meals; visible ingredients vary and never establish an allergen claim.

The vegan-wrap photo shows hummus, cucumber, tomato and carrot on an open tortilla. It provides a plant-based serving idea for vegetable wraps and chickpea pitas without the visible cheese in the other wrap photo.
