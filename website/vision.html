Future vision / beyond the screen

Your day could set
the world in motion.

Imagine leaving your last meeting and coming home to dinner ready, groceries put away, and a clean kitchen. Your schedule could help coordinate the services and devices that make it happen.

Future concepts, not available features.

Zelos does not currently connect to smart glasses, cars, or humanoid robots, place grocery orders, or coordinate autonomous pickup, cooking, or cleanup. The examples below explore what future integrations could make possible.

  1. 01 / Plan

    A week that fits you.

    With your permission, Zelos could bring together your schedule, selected health records, dietary preferences, and budget to suggest meals, preparation times, and a grocery list for your review.

  2. 02 / Shop & collect

    The ingredients, on their way.

    A future ordering connection could place an approved grocery order for delivery or pickup. Where a service supports it, an autonomous vehicle and a capable robot could collect the groceries together and bring them home.

  3. 03 / Prepare

    Ready before you need it.

    Your schedule could become a set of timed instructions for a compatible humanoid robot: receive a delivery, put ingredients away, set out what is needed, and start preparing your selected recipes before you arrive.

  4. 04 / Cook & clean

    Dinner ready. Kitchen reset.

    A robot with the necessary cooking and cleaning abilities could prepare the approved meals, portion the week’s meal prep, put leftovers away, and clean up after itself. You would choose the tasks, timing, and limits.

Future integration / smart glasses

Your day,
right in view.

Imagine bringing Zelos to smart glasses such as Even Realities G2. A future companion on your phone could put the context you choose in view, so you could keep your attention on the conversation, the errand, or the meal in front of you.

Proposed Zelos integration. A compatible companion app, permissions, and device testing would be needed. Zelos does not currently connect to Even G2.

  • Walk into a meeting prepared.

    Glance at your next appointment, a brief you selected, or the follow-up you wanted to remember.

  • Keep the grocery list within sight.

    Bring up the ingredients from your reviewed meal plan while you shop, with the details you chose to share.

  • Keep your hands on the meal.

    See the next recipe step while you prepare dinner, instead of repeatedly reaching for another screen.

  • Catch a thought while it is fresh.

    With microphone permission, a future companion could let you ask Zelos a quick question or capture a follow-up to review.

Your next stop, already in navigation.

A future vehicle integration could send a destination from your calendar to a compatible car, such as a Tesla, with your permission. Sending a destination is separate from driving: Tesla’s Full Self-Driving (Supervised) requires an attentive driver. Unattended grocery pickup would need a separately supported autonomous service.

One evening. So many possibilities.

The same idea could extend to preparing the house for guests, organizing a trip, or getting a workspace ready before your first call. Zelos could connect what you have planned with what needs to be ready, through devices and services you choose.

Any such integrations would need compatible hardware and services, their own operating limits, and your permission for spending and shared information. A robot would need the selected recipe and task instructions, rather than your full health history.
