How it works

Your context.
AI that can use it.

Zelos is the workspace around the AI. It brings in records, finds relevant context, gives that context to your chosen model, and puts useful results where you can review them.

  1. 01

    Connect what matters.

    Add the mail accounts, calendars, project sources, and local files you want Zelos to read. It keeps an archive on your computer. You choose the sources; Zelos checks them while it is open.

  2. 02

    Give AI the relevant background.

    For a question or check, Zelos gathers context from your records and asks the model you configured to interpret it. Instead of manually copying a thread into a chat, you can ask in the place that already holds the context.

  3. 03

    Turn the answer into a next step.

    See priorities, review meeting prep, follow source references, or inspect an assigned task’s result. Edit a draft, correct a detail, snooze an item, or mark it done. AI helps prepare the work; you decide what to trust and act on.

Your mail, calendar & notes → Zelos finds the context → Your chosen AI → A result to review

Future vision / beyond the screen

Your day could set
the world in motion.

Imagine leaving your last meeting and coming home to dinner ready, groceries put away, and a clean kitchen. Your schedule could help coordinate the services and devices that make it happen.

A future concept, not an available feature.

Zelos does not currently connect to cars or humanoid robots, place grocery orders, or coordinate autonomous pickup, cooking, or cleanup. The scenario below explores what future integrations could make possible.

  1. 01 / Plan

    A week that fits you.

    With your permission, Zelos could bring together your schedule, selected health records, dietary preferences, and budget to suggest meals, preparation times, and a grocery list for your review.

  2. 02 / Shop & collect

    The ingredients, on their way.

    A future ordering connection could place an approved grocery order for delivery or pickup. Where a service supports it, an autonomous vehicle and a capable robot could collect the groceries together and bring them home.

  3. 03 / Prepare

    Ready before you need it.

    Your schedule could become a set of timed instructions for a compatible humanoid robot: receive a delivery, put ingredients away, set out what is needed, and start preparing your selected recipes before you arrive.

  4. 04 / Cook & clean

    Dinner ready. Kitchen reset.

    A robot with the necessary cooking and cleaning abilities could prepare the approved meals, portion the week’s meal prep, put leftovers away, and clean up after itself. You would choose the tasks, timing, and limits.

Your next stop, already in navigation.

A future vehicle integration could send a destination from your calendar to a compatible car, such as a Tesla, with your permission. Sending a destination is separate from driving: Tesla’s Full Self-Driving (Supervised) requires an attentive driver. Unattended grocery pickup would need a separately supported autonomous service.

One evening. So many possibilities.

The same idea could extend to preparing the house for guests, organizing a trip, or getting a workspace ready before your first call. Zelos could connect what you have planned with what needs to be ready, through devices and services you choose.

Any such integrations would need compatible hardware and services, their own operating limits, and your permission for spending and shared information. A robot would need the selected recipe and task instructions, rather than your full health history.

Your AI, on your terms

Choose the model.
Keep the workspace.

Zelos does not lock your records to one AI provider. Configure a compatible online model, or run one locally, and keep working in the same place.

An online model

Use a compatible provider with your own API key and configuration. Zelos sends the context needed for a request to that provider.

This gives you access to hosted models without running one on your own hardware. Provider charges, capabilities, and data policies apply.

A model on your computer

Use a compatible model through Ollama, LM Studio, or a server on your local network. Personal-record questions, document review, and health planning in the preview require this kind of connection.

You control the model connection. Its own routing determines where it processes requests; a local address alone does not guarantee offline processing. Speed and answer quality depend on the model and hardware.

Useful help. Decisions that stay yours.

The model helps interpret, summarize, prioritize, and prepare work from the context Zelos provides. It can miss details or get an answer wrong. Check source references and review results before relying on them. The workspace also gives you direct search, calendars, records, and task controls that do not need an AI answer for every click.

In the {{PREVIEW_VERSION}} preview, email sending requires review and your explicit Send reply action. You choose which imported records to save and which items to share with a store or family member. Other AI clients receive only the read access you enable. Connected services still use their own networks.

How is Zelos different from opening a chat with an AI? +

Zelos combines a local archive, connected sources, and everyday views with an AI model you choose. You can ask about saved records without rebuilding all the background in each conversation, then keep working with tasks, calendars, drafts, and plans in the same workspace.

The AI model supplies language and reasoning capabilities. Zelos supplies the context, the workflow, and the controls around the result.

Does Zelos include an AI subscription? +

No. Zelos is free and open source. For an online model, you configure a compatible provider and may pay that provider for API usage. A chat subscription does not necessarily include API usage.

You can instead configure a local model. It needs suitable hardware and a compatible local endpoint. The website demo uses prepared examples and has no AI usage charge.

Does it automatically do things on my behalf? +

You can assign supported work, such as researching saved records, preparing an unsent reply, saving a note, or creating a weekly report. Zelos records the steps so you can inspect the result.

Sending email is a reviewed action in the current build. It does not silently complete a grocery checkout. Booking publication and external connections require their own setup.

Where do my records and AI requests go? +

The archive lives on your computer. Connected sources contact their own services. When you choose an online AI, the relevant content for the request goes to that provider.

A model served locally can process on your hardware, but its own configuration controls any onward routing. The preview also offers optional bank, search, store, family, and booking connections. See the preview’s data flows →
